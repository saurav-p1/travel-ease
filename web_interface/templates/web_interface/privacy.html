Privacy Policy

Last updated: {% now "F j, Y" %}

What we collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and (optionally) phone number and travel preferences. While you use TravelEase, we keep track of the destinations and restaurants you view and save, and any ratings you submit, so we can show you your own history and saved items. We also log basic login activity (timestamp, IP address, browser) for account security.

How we use it

Your information is used to run your account (logging you in, showing your saved items and recent views, letting you rate restaurants) and, only if you opt in, to send marketing emails about travel tips and offers. We don't sell your personal information to third parties.

Cookies

TravelEase uses a session cookie to keep you signed in and a CSRF cookie to protect forms from cross-site request forgery. These are functional cookies required for the site to work -- we don't use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

Your choices

You can update or delete your saved items and recent-view history at any time from your account. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails from your account preferences. To request deletion of your account entirely, contact us.

Third-party sign-in

If you sign up or log in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google to create or match your TravelEase account. We don't post to your Google account or access anything beyond that basic profile information.

Questions

If you have questions about how your data is handled, get in touch.
